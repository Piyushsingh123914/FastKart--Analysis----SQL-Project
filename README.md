## Questions

## Basic

* Retrieve the total number of customers by gender and role.
* Identify the users who have made the highest number of purchases.
* Identify the top 10 customers by total spend.
* Identify the products that generated the most revenue.
* Find the products that have zero sales.
* List the top 5 categories by revenue generated.
* Calculate the average price of products in each category.
* List the products with the lowest stock levels.
* Determine the day of the week with the highest sales volume.
* Calculate the monthly sales trend for the last 12 months.
* Compare male and female customers on number of purchases and total spend.
* Compare the number of purchases made on weekends versus weekdays.

## Advanced

* Calculate the percentage of users who are repeat buyers.
* Identify the users who have purchased from every category.
* Calculate the average interval (in days) between purchases for each user.
* Find the best-selling product in each category, using a window function.
* Calculate the revenue share (%) of each category.
* Identify the categories that are growing fastest month over month, using `LAG`.
* Find the products whose sales have declined for at least 3 consecutive months.
* List the top 5 customers by spend for every year, using `RANK`.
* Classify users into high, medium and low value segments, using `NTILE`.
* Identify the customers who increased their spend year over year.
* Calculate the percentage of total revenue that comes from the top 10 customers.
* Calculate the percentage of total revenue that comes from repeat customers.
